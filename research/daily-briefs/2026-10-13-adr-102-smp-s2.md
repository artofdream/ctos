# 2026-10-13 — ADR-102: SMP slice S2 (opt-in)

- **Sponsor go** for S2 at $0 (2026-10-13 about 22:45 CEST, after #154 / ADR-100 S1 merged), relayed by DSO.
- **What landed (opt-in `smp2-probe` only):** core 1 turns the MMU on with core 0's MAIR/TCR/TTBR0/TTBR1, runs from the TTBR1 high alias on its own 16 KiB stack, installs its own vector table, clears SPAN and sets PAN itself, takes one `brk #0x5102` that its own handler records (PAN=1 at entry) and returns. Both cores print through the `UART` lock, one whole line per hold; core 0 gives the verdict.
- **Negatives caught:** `-smp 1`; `smp2-neg-pan`; `smp2-neg-span`; `smp2-neg-vbar`.
- **Not claimed:** SMP support or safety; EL0 on two cores; TLB coherence; that the lock is necessary. ADR-091 rev 4 stays one core. `_start` untouched. Default build and required checks unchanged.
- **Deviation:** PerCpu via `TPIDR_EL1` moved to S4.
- **Next (proposed, needs a go):** S3 — type-enforced `TABLES`, Inner-Shareable TLBI, cross-core unmap probe with a local-TLBI negative.
